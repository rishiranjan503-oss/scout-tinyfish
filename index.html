How Scout Works

Scout uses three TinyFish APIs that work together to discover, read, and verify real job openings from live career pages.

1
TinyFish Search

Discovers live career pages on Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby, and company sites based on your role, location, and keywords.

2
TinyFish Fetch

Reads full page content, extracting job descriptions, compensation, stack requirements, and other details from the DOM.

3
TinyFish Agent

LLM reasoning verifies each job is real and currently open, calculates match scores, and removes duplicate postings.
